The fourteen

Every peak above 8,000 metres.

Landmark feats

Records that changed the sport.

1986

All fourteen

Reinhold Messner becomes the first person to climb all fourteen 8,000-metre peaks, completing the list with Lhotse.

2013

Samina Baig on Everest

Samina Baig becomes the first Pakistani woman to summit Mount Everest.

2016

Nanga Parbat in winter

Muhammad Ali Sadpara, Simone Moro and Alex Txikon make the first winter ascent of Nanga Parbat.

2019

Fourteen in six months

Nirmal Purja climbs all fourteen peaks in six months and six days, shattering the previous record of nearly eight years.

      Questions

      Worth knowing.

      Why 8,000 metres?

      It marks the height where the air holds roughly a third of the oxygen at sea level, the region climbers call the death zone.

      Who counts as having climbed all fourteen?

      Messner was first in 1986. Others have since repeated it, and debates continue about which routes and summits count.

      Which of the fourteen is the lowest?

      Shishapangma, at 8,027 metres, entirely within Tibet.
