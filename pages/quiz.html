How is the style quiz scored?

Each answer adds points to Alpine, Ice or Expedition. The style with the most points is your result.

Is my score saved?

Your best safety score is stored in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I retake the quizzes?

Yes, as many times as you like. Use the Retake button on the result screen.
