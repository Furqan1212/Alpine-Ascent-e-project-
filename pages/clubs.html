    Choosing wisely

    How to choose a club or guide.

    The right team matters as much as the right mountain. Ask these four questions before you book.

    • AccreditationLook for guides certified by a national association affiliated with the IFMGA, the international guides federation.
    • Safety recordAsk about past incidents, the rescue plan, and how often the team turns back in poor weather.
    • RatiosA smaller number of climbers per guide means more attention and faster decisions on the mountain.
    • Local staffChoose operators that pay porters and Sherpa guides fairly and provide proper kit and insurance.

    Questions

    Worth knowing.

    Do I need to be a member to climb with a club?

    Usually yes. Membership often includes insurance, hut discounts and access to courses and meets.

    How do I find the nearest club?

    Use the "Find clubs near me" button above. If you decline location access, search by country or region instead.

    What is the IFMGA?

    The International Federation of Mountain Guides Associations. Its certified guides meet high international training standards.
