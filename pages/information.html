Mountaineering

What is mountaineering?

Mountaineering — mountain climbing — is the sport of reaching, or attempting to reach, high points in mountainous regions, chiefly for the pleasure of the climb.

The term is often loosely applied to walking up low hills, but it properly belongs where terrain and weather make previous experience essential for safety. Unlike most sports, it has no widely-applied formal rules or governing body: climbers follow a rich variety of techniques and philosophies.

  • DisciplineAlpine, rock, ice & expedition
  • GovernanceSelf-regulated; ethics-led
  • Essential skillsNavigation, rope work, weather reading
  • Best practiceTrain, acclimatise, turn back in time

Anatomy of a climb

From idea to summit.

Every successful climb follows the same five stages. Skipping one is how accidents start.

1

Plan

Choose a mountain that matches your skills, check the season, book permits and build a training plan months ahead. Tell someone your route.

2

Approach

Walk in with a loaded pack, set up camp and check your gear. Easy days now save energy later.

3

Acclimatise

Climb high and sleep low over several days so your body can adapt to the thin air. Rest days are part of the plan.

4

Summit push

Start early, move steadily, and obey your turn-around time. The weather window decides everything.

5

Descent

Most accidents happen on the way down, when you are tired. Stay focused until you are back at camp.

Essential gear

What every climber carries.

Good gear does not replace skill, but poor gear can end a trip quickly.

  • BootsStiff, insulated and fitted over thick socks
  • LayersBase, insulation and a waterproof shell
  • Ice axe and cramponsFor snow and ice, with training to use them
  • Helmet and harnessAgainst rockfall and for roped travel
  • NavigationMap, compass, altimeter and a charged phone or radio

Questions

Worth knowing.

What is the difference between hiking and mountaineering?

Hiking follows paths. Mountaineering involves steep, exposed terrain where rope, ice and navigation skills are needed.

How do I start?

Begin with hill walking, take a basic mountaineering course, then join experienced partners or a guided climb.

Do I need to be very fit?

Good fitness helps, but skills and judgement keep you safe. Train endurance and learn to move efficiently.
