Before you write

Common questions.

Do I need experience to join an expedition?

Not for every trip. Beginner treks need good fitness and a willingness to learn, while technical peaks require rope, ice and altitude experience. Tell us your level in the form and we will point you to a suitable route.

What is the best season to climb in the Himalaya and Karakoram?

Spring (April to May) and autumn (September to October) are the main windows in Nepal. In the Karakoram, including K2 and Nanga Parbat in Pakistan, the main season is summer, from June to August.

How fit do I need to be?

Plan on three to six months of aerobic training, with loaded uphill walks. Altitude affects everyone differently, so fitness helps but does not guarantee acclimatisation.
