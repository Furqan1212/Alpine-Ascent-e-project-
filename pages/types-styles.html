Styles

Three ways up.

Each style demands its own gear, fitness and judgement. Most climbers blend several across a career.

Snow & Glacier

Alpine climbing

Mixed routes of rock, snow and ice, climbed light and fast in a single push from hut to summit.

Cold & Vertical

Ice climbing

Frozen waterfalls and glacial walls, climbed with ice axes and crampons.

Long & Committed

Expedition style

Multi-week campaigns on the great ranges, with fixed camps and slow acclimatisation.

Questions

Worth knowing.

Which style is best for a beginner?

Alpine routes on smaller peaks are a good start, with a guide and a basic course.

Do I need different gear for each style?

Some gear overlaps, such as harness, helmet and layers, but ice tools and expedition equipment are specialised.

How do I choose?

Match your available time, fitness and comfort with cold, or take the style quiz for a suggestion.
