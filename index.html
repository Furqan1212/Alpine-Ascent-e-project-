01 / 06

Base Camp

5,364 m · 51% oxygen · about -10°C

Six weeks of waiting. Teams rest, eat and acclimatise while Sherpa climbers fix ropes through the icefall above.

02 / 06

Camp 1

6,065 m · 46% oxygen · about -15°C

Above the Khumbu Icefall, a shifting maze of ice towers. Teams cross it before dawn, when the cold holds it steady.

03 / 06

Camp 2

6,500 m · 43% oxygen · about -18°C

The Western Cwm traps heat like an oven by day and freezes hard at night. Climbers sleep here to adapt to the thin air.

04 / 06

Camp 3

7,200 m · 39% oxygen · about -25°C

Tents are pinned to the steep Lhotse Face. Most climbers start breathing bottled oxygen from about here.

05 / 06

Camp 4 · South Col

7,906 m · 36% oxygen · about -30°C

The final camp sits inside the death zone. Teams rest a few hours, then leave near midnight for the summit.

06 / 06

Summit

8,849 m · 31% oxygen · about -36°C

The top of the world. Climbers stay only minutes before the long descent, where most accidents happen.
